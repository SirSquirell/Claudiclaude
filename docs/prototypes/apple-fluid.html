US-55 Grab the chart to set the range

Drag a handle: it sticks to your finger, rubber-bands past the ends, and on release settles with a critically-damped spring — a flick throws it, momentum projecting where it lands and snapping to a day. Grab it mid-settle and it reverses from where it is. Hover for the crosshair. The Result recomputes every frame from the real span — no smoothing of the data.

US-56 Response, and honest degradation

Everything highlights on pointer-down, not release — press and hold, then drag away to cancel. Flip the switches to simulate the OS accessibility settings: reduced-motion keeps the tracking but drops the overshoot; reduced-transparency frosts the glass solid; more-contrast borders the controls.

US-57 The share sheet as a material

Open it: the card materializes — blur and scale spring in together, so it reads as glass arriving, not a fade. The four formats are a momentum strip — flick and it snaps, and the card reshapes to the format. Content is frozen (that's US-52/54); this is only the glass.
